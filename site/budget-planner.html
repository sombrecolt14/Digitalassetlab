From a blank project to a client report.

The planner works in the order a studio already does.

  1. 01

    Choose the spaces

    Home or office, how many bedrooms, bathrooms and cabins, plus anything else the project needs: a pooja room, a study, a gym.

  2. 02

    See where the money goes

    The plan by spend draws every space at its share of the budget, shaded by what it costs, with a list that ranks them and shows what is still open.

  3. 03

    Work room by room

    Each room has its plan, its finishes and a checklist of what usually goes in it. Add each line, mark it not needed, or leave it for later.

  4. 04

    Doors and windows, wall by wall

    Give the room its size and openings. Wall areas, paint and skirting come out right, less the real doors and windows.

  5. 05

    Every item drawn to size

    Pick a straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, island or parallel kitchen and see it drawn. Wardrobes, ceilings, lighting and joinery too, 193 items in all.

  6. 06

    Nothing forgotten

    The budget check lists what a project like this usually needs and you have not added, from switches to debris removal, and what still needs a price.

  7. 07

    A wood list for your carpenter

    Board, laminate and edge banding to buy, in sheets and running feet, worked out from the sizes of your joinery.

  8. 08

    A report your client can read

    The total against their budget and every room with its items, under your studio's name and logo. Print it or save it as a PDF.

Questions

Anything else, write to us from the Contact page.

Is it only for homes?

No. Choose home or office at the start. Offices and shops get reception, workspaces, cabins, conference rooms and pantries, with networking, fire safety and approvals in the checklist.

Does it have market rates built in?

No. You enter your own rates, so the budget reflects your vendors and your city. The rates and materials you use often are saved for the next project.

Can I give a quick figure before I have details?

Yes. Set one overall budget for a space and break it down later with its checklist.

Can my client see it?

Send them the client report as a PDF. You choose whether it shows rates, alternatives and notes.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. Updates are included.
