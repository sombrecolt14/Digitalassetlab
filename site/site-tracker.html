From the first photo to fixed.

Made for standing on site with a phone in one hand.

  1. 01

    A folder for every visit

    Today's folder starts itself with your first photo, and every photo and note from the visit goes in it, room by room. It all saves on the phone first and syncs when you are signed in.

  2. 02

    Circle the problem

    Tap a photo to circle what needs fixing, or draw on it. Record a short video, up to 60 seconds, and keep the moment that matters as a still.

  3. 03

    Who fixes it, and by when

    Each note gets the room, the trade, the priority and a due date. To fix, in progress, fixed, with anything overdue at the top.

  4. 04

    Lists on WhatsApp

    Send the site supervisor everything still to fix, grouped by trade. Each contractor can get their own list too, in a tap.

  5. 05

    A report from your phone

    One PDF for a visit or the whole project, with before and after photos, shared straight from the phone. The client version leaves out your internal notes. Add a summary of the day, copy it for WhatsApp, or share a link.

  6. 06

    Every site, one tap away

    Each project keeps the site location, from a Google Maps link or from where you are standing, with what is overdue at a glance. Share a project with your team.
