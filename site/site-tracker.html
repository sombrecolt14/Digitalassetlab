What it will do.

Made for standing on site with a phone in one hand.

Visits

Start a visit in one tap. The project, date, time and who visited are filled in.

Snags with photos

A photo, a line about it, the room, who fixes it, the priority and the date it is due.

Follow-up

Open, in progress, fixed. Overdue snags rise to the top, and you can filter by room or trade.

Before and after

Photograph the problem, then the fix. The client sees both, and you keep a record.

Visit reports

A short report for the client or contractor, with the photos, to share as a PDF.

Offline on site

Log visits and photos with no signal. Everything uploads when the phone is back online.
