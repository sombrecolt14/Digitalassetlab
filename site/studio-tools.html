Questions

Anything else, write to us from the Contact page.

When do they open?

When they are ready, not before. The Budget Planner and Invoice Generator are in final testing; the Site Tracker follows. Studios on the list are told first, with a few days to buy at the founding price.

What will they cost?

A one-time price for each tool, and less for all three together. No subscription. Studios on the list get a founding price.

Do I need to install anything?

No. They open in your browser on a computer, phone or tablet. You can also add them to your home screen so they open like an app.

Does it work without signal on site?

Yes. Keep working with no network; changes save on the device and sync when you are back online.

Is my clients' information private?

Projects are stored in your own account and only you can open them. You can download everything or delete your account at any time.
