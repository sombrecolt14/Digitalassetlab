Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Digital Asset Lab respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your information.

Information you provide

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Billing information and payment details (processed securely through Razorpay — we never store card details)
  • Order history and download records

Information collected automatically

  • IP address and browser type
  • Device information and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referring website addresses

How we use your information

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Sending download links and purchase confirmations
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Sending updates about new products and features (you can opt out anytime)
  • Improving our website and services
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

Who we share data with

  • Payment processors: Razorpay, for secure payment processing
  • Email service providers: for sending order confirmations and updates
  • Legal requirements: when required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell your personal data.

How we protect your data

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure payment processing through PCI-compliant Razorpay
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Restricted access to personal data

Your rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@digitalassetlab.in or our contact page.
