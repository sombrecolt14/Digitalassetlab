From the first advance to the last receipt.

One record per job, so you always know what was billed, what was paid and what is still due.

  1. 01

    Know what you are owed

    The Invoices page opens with what you are owed, what is overdue and what came in this month. Every proforma, tax invoice and draft is below, with a search, and any invoice duplicates in a tap.

  2. 02

    GST worked out for you

    Enter the client's GSTIN: it is checked, and the invoice knows their state. CGST and SGST in the same state, IGST in another, and the SAC code follows the service you pick.

  3. 03

    Charge the way you quote

    Each line can be per square foot, a percentage of project cost, a lump sum, per item, per visit, per hour or per month, and the invoice shows the working.

  4. 04

    Get paid, send the receipt

    Record advances and part payments, send a numbered receipt for each, and put a UPI QR code on the invoice for the amount due. When a payment is late, copy a reminder for WhatsApp or email.

  5. 05

    The GST you owe

    Add your supplier bills with the vendor's GSTIN. The input tax credit comes off the GST you charged, by month or by quarter, and your CA gets a spreadsheet. It is a working figure, not a return.

  6. 06

    What each client owes

    Each client has a page with every invoice, what they have paid and what they still owe.

Questions

Anything else, write to us from the Contact page.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is an invoicing tool that follows the GST rules for invoices. Check your SAC codes and rates with your CA once, and the tool applies them every time.

I am not registered for GST. Can I use it?

Yes. Choose unregistered and it makes a bill of supply without tax, with the same payments and receipts.

Can I bill a client in another state, or abroad?

Yes. Another state uses IGST automatically. For clients abroad, choose export, with or without an LUT.

Can I use it on my phone and my laptop?

Yes. Sign in on each device and your invoices are there, with backups. A Help page and a guided tour show you around.

Does it do e-invoicing?

Not yet. E-invoicing on the government portal applies above a turnover most design studios do not reach.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. Updates are included.
