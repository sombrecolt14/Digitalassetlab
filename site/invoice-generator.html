Questions

Anything else, write to us from the Contact page.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is an invoicing tool that follows the GST rules for invoices. Check your SAC codes and rates with your CA once, and the tool applies them every time.

I am not registered for GST. Can I use it?

Yes. Choose unregistered and it makes a bill of supply without tax, with the same payments and receipts.

Can I bill a client in another state, or abroad?

Yes. Another state uses IGST automatically. For clients abroad, choose export, with or without an LUT.

Does it do e-invoicing?

Not yet. E-invoicing on the government portal applies above a turnover most design studios do not reach.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once. Updates are included.
